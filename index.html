01 / About

Curiosity with purpose.

A student-led research journey rooted in computer science, interdisciplinary thinking, and continuous learning.

I am Samin Yesar Tousib, an undergraduate student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Pabna University of Science and Technology (PUST), Bangladesh.

My academic interests center on artificial intelligence and machine learning, especially their use in privacy-aware systems, visual understanding, spectroscopy and sensor data analysis. I am interested in connecting computational methods with practical scientific problems.

Outside academics, I value sports, teamwork, problem-solving, and opportunities to grow through collaboration.
